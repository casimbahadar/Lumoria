❓ How to Play Lumoria

🗺️ Exploring the World

Use the world map to travel between towns, routes, and landmarks. Click any location to select it, then press Explore Area to enter and encounter wild Lumos.

⚔️ Battles

When you encounter a wild Lumo a battle begins. Choose FIGHT to use a move, CATCH to throw an Orb, SWITCH to swap your active Lumo, or RUN to flee. Moves with higher power deal more damage — watch out for type advantages!

🔵 Type Advantages

Each Lumo and move has a type (Fire, Water, Grass, Electric…). Using a move that's super effective against the opponent's type deals double damage. Not very effective moves deal half. Plan your team around type coverage!

🥚 Catching Lumos

Open the BAG from the bottom nav to see your Orbs. In battle, press CATCH and choose an Orb. Weakening the wild Lumo first (lower HP = higher catch chance) and inflicting a status condition like Sleep or Paralysis greatly increases your odds.

👥 Your Team

You can carry up to 6 Lumos. Open TEAM in the bottom nav to view stats, moves, and HP. Use Potions from your Bag to heal between battles. If all your Lumos faint you'll black out and return to the last town.

🏛️ Gyms & Progression

Each town with a Gym has a Gym Leader specialising in a specific type. Defeat them to earn a Badge. Collect all 8 Badges to unlock Victory Road and challenge the Champion. Badges are shown in the top-left HUD.

📖 Lumordex

Open the LUMODEX to see every Lumo you have seen or caught. Completing your Dex by catching all Lumos is the ultimate challenge!

🎗️ Held Items

Lumos can hold items for stat boosts and battle effects! Open TEAM, select a Lumo, and equip a held item from the Held Item section. Earn held items by defeating Gym Leaders. Items like Charcoal boost moves of a specific type, Power Band raises Attack, Focus Sash lets you survive a fatal hit, and Leftovers heals HP each turn.

💾 Saving

Press SAVE in the bottom nav at any time. Your progress is stored in your browser's local storage — it persists between sessions as long as you use the same browser.

### What counts as an obtainability path

Only battles where catching is enabled grant a Lumori. Verified against `game.js`:
- **Starter** selection (ids 1, 4, 7).
- **Wild** random encounters (`wildMonsters[]`).
- **NG+ Wild** encounters (`ngPlusWildMonsters[]`, only after a New Game+).
- **Static legendary** encounters (`legendaryEncounter` — `isLegendary:true`, catch enabled).
- **Evolution** from an already-obtainable Lumori (any `evolveTo` method: level / item / location).

**Not** obtainability paths (catch is disabled — `isWild:false`): quest `boss` battles, gym/champion/rival battles, and `UMBRA_BATTLES` enemy teams. A Lumori that appears *only* as a quest boss or Umbra enemy is **uncatchable**. There are no trade, gift, egg/breeding, or fossil mechanics in the codebase.
